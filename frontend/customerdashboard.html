Everything you need to know about using PinkMeUp. If you need further assistance, our team is one click away.

Quick Search

The search bar at the top adapts to whatever you're doing. Press Ctrl + K (or ⌘ + K on Mac) to jump to it from anywhere.

On Home it searches across everything — appointments, services, and stylists.

On My Appointments it searches your appointments only.

On Book Appointment it searches services first, then stylists.

Booking an Appointment

1

Open the menu and tap Book Appointment, or use the "Book now" link on the Home page.

2

Select your desired services from the categories. Use the search bar to find them quickly.

3

Pick a date, choose a stylist, then select an available time. If no stylist matches your selection, we'll assign one for you.

4

Enter your contact details, review your booking, and confirm.

Managing Appointments

Every appointment lives under My Appointments. From there you can:

Filter by All, Upcoming, Past, or Cancelled — or filter by a specific date.

Tap any appointment to view full details, reschedule, or cancel.

Rate a completed visit with a star rating and optional comment.

Notifications

The bell at the top right shows reminders for upcoming appointments, prompts to rate completed visits, and confirmations of new bookings. Tap any notification to jump straight to the relevant appointment.
