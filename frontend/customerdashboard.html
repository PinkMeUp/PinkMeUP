Help & Navigation Guide

Quick Search

Press Ctrl + K (or ⌘ + K on Mac) to jump to the search bar. Search across your appointments and services in one place.

Booking an Appointment

1

Click "Book" in the sidebar or "Book now" in the header.

2

Select your desired services from the categories. Use search to find them quickly.

3

Pick a date, choose a stylist, then select an available time.

4

Enter your contact details and review your booking.

Struggling with online booking or have a query? Call us at 076 769 0102

Managing Appointments

• View all appointments under "My Appointments".

• Use filter buttons (All, Upcoming, Past, Cancelled) to sort.

• Search by service, stylist or date.

• Click on an appointment to view details and manage it.
