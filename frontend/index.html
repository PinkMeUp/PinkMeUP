Our Story

About PinkMeUp

Beauty, crafted with heart — from the heart of Braamfontein.

PM
Phumuzile Moyo

Founder & Lead Therapist

PinkMeUp Beauty Spa & Academy was born from a simple belief — that world-class self-care should feel personal, unhurried, and genuinely warm. What started as a mobile beauty service has grown into a full spa and accredited academy inside Wits University, Matrix Building, Floor 1.

Founded in 2021 by self-taught entrepreneur Phumuzile Moyo, PinkMeUp now offers luxury facials, nail treatments, lash artistry, massage therapy, and waxing, alongside professional training accredited by QCTO and SDCC Canada.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when booking or registering — such as your name, email address, phone number, and appointment preferences. We also automatically collect basic technical data like your IP address and browser type to keep the site secure and improve performance.

2
How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to confirm bookings, send appointment reminders, process payments, and improve our services. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

3
Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data using secure servers and encryption. However, no online transmission is ever 100% secure, so we encourage you to keep your account credentials private.

Terms of Service

The ground rules for using PinkMeUp services.

Last updated: 18 August 2026
1
Bookings & Confirmation

All appointments are confirmed once you receive a booking confirmation via email or SMS. Please arrive 5–10 minutes early. Late arrivals may result in shortened treatment time.

2
Cancellations & Rescheduling

We kindly ask for at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations or no-shows may be subject to a fee, or loss of deposit where applicable.

3
Payments

Services are payable at the time of booking or on the day of your appointment. Academy fees follow the agreed payment plan. All prices are in South African Rand (ZAR) and may be updated from time to time.

4
Health & Safety

Please inform us of any allergies, skin conditions, or medical concerns before your treatment. We reserve the right to refuse service where it may pose a health risk to you or our staff.

5
Academy Enrolments

Academy programmes are subject to registration, deposit, and starter kit requirements. Course fees are non-transferable. Certification is awarded upon successful completion of all modules.

6
Contact

For any questions about these terms, please contact us at pinkmeup01@gmail.com or call 076 769 0102.
