Help & Support

Managing Services

Click New Service to add a service with its category, price, and duration.

Use the edit icon to update an existing service, or the trash icon to archive it from the customer view.

Managing Stylists

Click New Stylist to add a stylist and assign their service specialties.

Change a stylist's status directly from their row: Active, Inactive (on leave), or Disabled.

Bookings & Analytics

Mark appointments as complete or flag no-shows from the Bookings tab.

Analytics can be filtered by period and exported as a PDF report.
