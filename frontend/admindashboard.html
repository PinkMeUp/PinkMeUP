Managing Bookings

Every appointment lands in the Bookings section. You can:

  • Filter by time period (Today, This Week, This Month, or a custom range)
  • Filter by status using the pill tabs
  • Click any row to see the full details of a booking
  • Mark an appointment as Complete once the customer has been served
  • Flag a booking as a No-show if the customer didn't arrive
  • Download the schedule as PDF or print a paper copy

Managing Stylists

Your stylists are the heart of PinkMeUp. From the Stylists section you can:

  • Add a new stylist with their contact details and specialties
  • Edit existing stylists to change their status or specialties
  • Change a stylist's status between Active, Inactive, or Disabled
  • View performance ratings and manage their service offerings

Managing Services

Keep your service menu organised. In the Services section you can:

  • Add new services with category, price, and duration
  • Edit or delete existing services at any time
  • Search by service name or category
  • Download the full menu as a PDF grouped by category

Analytics & Reports

Understand how your salon is performing with detailed analytics. You can:

  • Filter data by Last 30 days, Last 90 days, Last 12 months, or a custom range
  • View booking trends by day of week and identify your busiest hours
  • See your most popular services and top-performing stylists
  • Download a full PDF report for your records
