Manage Services
Your salon's service menu is what customers see when they book with PinkMeUp. Add new services with their category, price, and duration, keep existing ones up to date, and archive anything you no longer offer. Everything is organised by category so the menu stays clean and easy to browse.
Click any service to view its full details and manage it.
Manage Stylists
Your stylists are the heart of PinkMeUp. Add new team members, assign the services they specialise in, and manage their availability so customers always see accurate options. Each stylist's status — Active, Inactive, or Disabled — controls whether they appear for bookings.
Click any stylist to open their full profile and manage them.
Manage Bookings
Every appointment booked at your salon lands here. From this list you can see the customer, requested services, assigned stylist, and current status at a glance. Open any booking to view the full details and take action — mark it as complete when the customer has been served, or flag it as a no-show if they didn't arrive. Completed and cancelled bookings are locked to preserve the record.
Click any booking to view it in full.
